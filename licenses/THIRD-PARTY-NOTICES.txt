Joyeer third-party notices
=========================

The native backend embeds LLVM and LLD 22.1.8. Linux builds also embed the
matching Clang Driver library. Windows builds embed LibXml2 2.14.5.
These components are not modified by Joyeer.

LLVM-LICENSE.txt and LLD-LICENSE.txt reproduce the respective upstream
LICENSE.TXT files at the llvmorg-22.1.8 tag:
https://github.com/llvm/llvm-project/tree/llvmorg-22.1.8

LLVM-SUPPORT-NOTICES.txt contains additional notices for the bundled regular
expression implementation, xxHash, Unicode conversions, and MD5.
BLAKE3-LICENSE.txt reproduces llvm/lib/Support/BLAKE3/LICENSE at the same tag.
Linux packages additionally include Clang-LICENSE.txt from clang/LICENSE.TXT.

Windows packages include LibXml2-Copyright.txt from the pinned 2.14.5 source
archive and LibXml2-SUPPLEMENTAL-NOTICES.txt for dict.c and list.c:
https://download.gnome.org/sources/libxml2/2.14/libxml2-2.14.5.tar.xz

GoogleTest, LLVM executables, development headers, and platform SDKs are not
part of the product package. The host compiler and platform SDK are external
prerequisites. Custom LLVM SDKs that embed additional dependencies need their
corresponding redistribution notices as well.
